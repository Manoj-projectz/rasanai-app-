Good to know

Questions, answered

How does RasanAi import a recipe from YouTube? +
Paste a YouTube video or Short link into the app. The AI reads the video description and captions, then returns a clean recipe with ingredients, quantities and step by step instructions. If a video has no recipe text, it tells you honestly.
Can it really read handwriting? +
Yes. Snap a photo of a recipe card or a notebook page and RasanAi transcribes it, structures it, and generates a warm food photo to go with it. It reads handwriting across all six languages, and gives you the finished recipe in every one of them.
Which languages are supported? +
Every recipe comes in six languages: English, Hindi, Tamil, Malayalam, Telugu and Kannada. You can set the whole app to your language too.
Is it free? +
You get fifteen free recipe imports to start. RasanAi Pro unlocks unlimited imports, the AI cooking assistant, the meal planner, the smart shopping list, and the tools to create and share your own recipes.
Can I create my own recipes? +
Yes, with RasanAi Pro. Speak your recipe out loud or type it in any of the six languages, and RasanAi structures it into a clean, editable format. It lives in your Created collection, right alongside everything you import and scan.
Can I share the recipes I create? +
Yes. Share any recipe as a beautiful branded RasanAi card. Sharing is private and link only, so it reaches exactly who you choose. There is no public feed, and your recipes stay yours.
Does it work for Instagram or TikTok? +
For now RasanAi imports from YouTube, which is the most reliable source. For an Instagram or TikTok recipe, take a screenshot of the caption and use the Scan feature. It reads the text for you.

Delete Your Account

How to permanently delete your RasanAi account and data

Testware Informatics Pvt Ltd. support@rasanai.app

RasanAi ("we", "our", "the App") is built and operated by Testware Informatics Pvt Ltd. This page explains how to delete your RasanAi account, and what data is removed or retained when you do.

Delete your account from inside the app

The fastest way to delete your account is directly in the RasanAi app:

  1. Open the RasanAi app and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap "Delete my account".
  4. Confirm by tapping "Delete Account" in the dialog.

Your account and all associated cloud data are permanently deleted immediately. This action cannot be undone.

Prefer to request it by email?

If you cannot access the app, email us from the address linked to your account and we will delete it for you:

Email support@rasanai.app with the subject "Delete my RasanAi account". We will verify ownership and complete the deletion within 30 days (usually much sooner).

What data is deleted

When your account is deleted, the following are permanently and irreversibly removed from our servers:

DataWhat happens
Your account and sign-in identity (Google / Apple)Deleted
Profile details (display name, language, preferences)Deleted
Cloud-synced saved recipes, meal plans and listsDeleted
Subscription and import-usage recordsDeleted
On-device data on the phone you usedCleared on sign-out

What may be retained

A limited amount of information may be retained only where required, and is not linked to your deleted account:

  • Billing and transaction records held by Apple App Store or Google Play, and the minimum records we must keep for tax and legal compliance under Indian law. These are managed by the respective app store and cannot be deleted by us.
  • Anonymous, aggregated diagnostics that cannot be used to identify you.

Managing your subscription is separate from deleting your account. To stop billing, cancel your subscription in your Apple App Store or Google Play account settings.

Questions about deletion or your data? Contact us any time at privacy@rasanai.app.

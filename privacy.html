RasanAi ("we", "our", "the App") is built and operated by Testware Informatics Pvt Ltd. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect when you use the RasanAi mobile application and website (rasanai.app), why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and what rights you have. By using RasanAi, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Who This Policy Applies To

This policy applies to all users of the RasanAi mobile app (iOS and Android) and the website at rasanai.app. If you are under 13 years of age, you may not use RasanAi. If you are between 13 and 18, a parent or guardian must review this policy on your behalf.

We collect only what is necessary to provide the service. The table below is our full disclosure:

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Provide the service — authenticate you, sync your recipes, process subscription entitlements
  • AI features — send recipe text and voice audio to OpenAI APIs to generate responses. We never send your phone number or account details to OpenAI.
  • Improve the App — aggregate, anonymised usage patterns to understand which features work best
  • Customer support — respond to your support requests
  • Legal obligations — comply with applicable Indian and international laws

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or selling to third parties.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Supabase infrastructure (hosted on AWS, Asia-Pacific region). We implement:

  • TLS 1.3 encryption for all data in transit
  • AES-256 encryption for data at rest
  • Row-Level Security (RLS) — your data is mathematically isolated from other users at the database level
  • No passwords stored — authentication is via OTP only (no password to steal)
  • API secrets stored in Cloudflare Workers environment — never exposed to the client app

Despite these measures, no system is 100% secure. We will notify you promptly in the event of a data breach that affects your personal information.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Active account: We retain your data as long as your account is active
  • Account deletion: When you delete your account (via Settings → Delete Account in the App), all your personal data is permanently deleted within 30 days
  • Voice audio: Not stored — processed in real-time and discarded immediately
  • Camera images: Not stored on our servers — processed by OpenAI and discarded
  • Subscription records: Retained for up to 7 years as required by Indian financial regulations (GST compliance)

To request deletion of your account and all associated data, go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account in the App, or email us at support@rasanai.app with subject "Data Deletion Request".

7. Your Rights

Under the Indian Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023:

  • Right to access the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to erase your data (right to be forgotten)
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time
  • Right to grievance redressal via our Grievance Officer
  • Right to nominate a person to exercise rights on your behalf

Under GDPR (if you are in the European Economic Area):

  • Right to data portability (receive your data in a machine-readable format)
  • Right to restrict processing
  • Right to object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority

Under CalOPPA / CCPA (if you are in California, USA):

  • Right to know what personal information is collected, used, shared or sold
  • Right to delete personal information
  • Right to opt-out of sale of personal information (we do not sell data)
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@rasanai.app. We will respond within 30 days.
